<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/multiples-power2816/1">Sum of Multiples of 3 or 7</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a natural number <strong>n</strong>, find the sum of all numbers strictly less than <strong>n</strong> that are multiples of 3 or 7.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 10
<strong>Output: </strong>25
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Numbers that are multiple of 3 or 7 are 3, 6, 7, 9 so sum will be 25.
</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 21
<strong>Output: </strong>84
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Numbers that are multiple of 3 or 7 are 3, 6, 7, 9, 12, 14, 15, 18 so sum will be 84.
</span></pre>
